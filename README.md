📊**Independence Day Participation Analysis Dashboard** 
An Excel-based data analytics project that analyzes Independence Day event participation across states, departments, event categories, genders, and attendance statuses.
The dashboard is built using Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, and interactive charts. It helps identify participation trends, event popularity, departmental engagement, gender distribution, and overall attendance performance.
The dataset contains 200 participant records across 6 states, 6 departments, and 6 Independence Day event categories.

📌 **Project Overview**
This project was created to analyze participant data for Independence Day activities and present the findings through a professional Excel dashboard.
The dashboard answers key business questions such as:
How many participants attended the events?
Which states had the highest participation?
Which events were most popular?
How balanced was male and female participation?
Which departments had the highest engagement?
What was the overall feedback rating?

🏆 **Key Insights**
Delhi and Maharashtra recorded the highest participation, with 40 participants each.
Kerala had the lowest participation, with 24 participants.
Cultural events received the highest participation, with 49 participants.
Rangoli received the lowest participation, with 21 participants.
Male participants numbered 103, while female participants numbered 97, showing a nearly balanced gender distribution.
Finance had the highest department-wise participation, with 40 participants.
Training had the lowest department-wise participation, with 28 participants.
Out of 200 participants, 143 participated, 30 were registered, and 27 were absent.
The average feedback rating was 4.05 out of 5, indicating positive participant response
